Tiny Thinkers

Gentle, colorful learning videos for toddlers (ages 2–5)

Tiny Thinkers makes short animated videos that teach one simple idea at a time: counting, colors, shapes, letters, animal sounds and kindness. Every video is calm, cheerful and made with young children in mind.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@LittleLearnersLab4

About the TinyThinker Uploader app

TinyThinker Uploader is a private tool used only by the Tiny Thinkers team. It creates our videos and uploads them to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's Google account.
