Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Tiny Thinkers YouTube channel and the TinyThinker Uploader app (the "App"), operated by Francisco Franco.

Who uses the App

The App is an internal tool used only by the Tiny Thinkers team to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public, and members of the public cannot sign in to it.

Google user data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App receives permission to use the YouTube Data API on the Tiny Thinkers channel. The App uses this access only to upload videos we create and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, privacy and "made for kids" settings. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, or any other Google user.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored securely in our automation platform (n8n) and is used only to upload our videos. Our own video files are stored in our private cloud storage (Amazon S3). We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Our videos are made for young children, but neither the App nor this website collects personal information from children or any viewer. Videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy practices apply to viewing on YouTube.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
